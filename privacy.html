Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

Eli Songz Content Automation is intended to manage content for the artist's authorized TikTok account. When the account owner authorizes the application, TikTok may provide account identification, authorization credentials, and permissions needed to upload content.

These details are used solely to authenticate the authorized account, prepare and publish approved videos, and troubleshoot publishing. Video files and metadata may be processed locally on the operator's computer. TikTok receives content submitted through its API.

Access credentials should be stored securely, not shared publicly, and revoked when no longer needed. The account owner can revoke access in TikTok's connected-app settings. Requests concerning stored data or access can be directed to the application operator.

This policy must be reviewed and updated to reflect actual storage, retention, any third-party processors, and a working support contact before the app is submitted for review.
